Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Halvora Studio ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the connected YouTube channels to manage their own channels.

What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner grants permission through Google sign-in, the tool accesses, via YouTube API Services:

  • the owner's channel information, videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails;
  • channel and video statistics from YouTube Analytics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and edit the owner's own videos and to review their performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private computer. The owner can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
